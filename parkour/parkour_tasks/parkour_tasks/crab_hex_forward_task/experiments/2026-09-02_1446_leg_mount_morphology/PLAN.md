## Question
The plant topples nose-down over its leading leg row (100% of ~148 recorded eval falls;
walking forward static tip angle ~18°/13° p50/p10 vs the 28.6° termination limit). Two
hardware levers on the front/rear leg mounts:
- **A — splay** (outward yaw, ≤ 20° so the ±25° cam throw can still reach perpendicular for
  sideways walking), implemented as an angled shim under the door-hinge leaf;
- **B — outboard axes** (yaw axes 5.5 in → 2.5 in from the body ends).
All 8 configurations (base, B, A10/15/20, A10/15/20+B) are evaluated; the user decides from
the final table. Splay-only is the user's stated preference (tie-breaker, not a prune).

## Conventions
- Generator row "F" is at −x; the robot walks +x → row **R leads**. Outward splay = R toes
  toward +x, F toes toward −x, symmetric about the transverse mid-plane and L/R.
- Positive pitch = nose-down. Termination = total tilt ≥ 0.5 rad (28.6°) or hip contact.
- Heading-frame stability metrics: `gait_eval/metrics.py::support_polygon_metrics`,
  `fall_direction_metrics` (new, unit-tested in `tests/unit/test_crab_hex_stability_metrics.py`).
- Leg FK: `crab_hexapod_task/mdp/crab_hex_foot_fk.py` (unit-tested; Isaac cross-check at rung ii).
