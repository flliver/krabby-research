# Policy summary — what the forward-walk policy of record was trained on, and why

Hand-maintained companion to [`README.md`](README.md) (which is generated from [`manifest.yaml`](manifest.yaml)
and holds the heads, sha256s and eval numbers of record). This document explains the training: what each
phase was for, which rewards, terrain and other configuration were active in it, and what every reward
term, terrain preset and non-reward knob does.

**Scope.** The A15+B lineage selected by `KRABBY_PHASE`: `1a -> 2a -> 2b -> 2c -> 3a`
(`config/crab_hex/crab_hex_phases.py`), plus the run-but-not-baked `3b`. The legacy bridge / 2b1 / 2b2 /
full stages of May–August 2026 are described in the task README §2 and are not covered here.

**Sources of truth are the code and the manifest, not this file.** Every table between
`<!-- generated:… -->` markers is regenerated by `experiments/tools/bundle_policy.py --sync` from the
presets (`crab_hex_phases.py`), the manifest, and [`mdp_pins.yaml`](mdp_pins.yaml) — a machine-extracted
copy of the Isaac config dumps that the config-identity test validates (see [§12](#12-maintaining-this-document)).
A unit test fails if any generated block is stale. The prose carries citations to the code
(`file::symbol`) and to the campaign records under `experiments/` (file + dated heading) it summarises.

**How to read.** *Phase* = preset name (`1a`, `2a`, …); *stage* = the folder in `policy/`
(`1a_formation/`, …); *window* = the lineage's 5 000-iteration index (window 0 = `1a`, …, window 3 = `2c`).
"Window start" weights are the values before that window's cosine ramps run; eps = 1e-3 is the floor a
ramped weight settles at (a weight of exactly 0 would make the reward manager skip the term). Evals are
written completion / falls out of 100 episodes / tripod score.

## 2. What is fixed across all phases (the MDP contract)

Everything in this section is identical in every phase of record, including the distillation phase — except
the mirror-symmetry loss, an RL-only learning term that distillation does not use, and the depth-camera observation groups (`depth_camera`, `delta_yaw_ok`) that only the student carries (§2.1, §2.2) — and is
what the 2c teacher and the 3a student share (pinned by
`tests/integration/test_crab_hex_phase_configs.py::test_student_phase_mirrors_2c_mdp`). The phases in
[§3](#3-training-phases) therefore list only what varies: reward weights, terrain, and the knobs.

### 2.1 Plant, tasks and modes

The plant is the A15+B robot (`assets/crab.usda`: 15° outward splay of the front and rear leg mounts, outer
yaw axes 2.5 in from the body ends; `docs/crab-hexapod-plant.md`), spawned at `KRABBY_HEX_SPAWN_Z` = 1.085 m.
Phase `1a` trains `Isaac-Crab-Hex-Flat-Walk-v0`; `2a`/`2b`/`2c` train `Isaac-Crab-Hex-Teacher-v0` in
teacher modes `2a`/`2b`/`2c`, which rebuild the flat-walk MDP inside the teacher task
(`crab_hex_env_cfg.py::_crab_hex_apply_teacher_mode`: the flat-walk rewards and terminations, the `full`
action term, then `apply_flat_walk_knobs`). The three modes differ from one another only through the
environment knobs — there is no per-mode code — and the identity test proves a Teacher-v0 env under a
preset equals the Flat-Walk-v0 env under the same preset. `3a`/`3b` train `Isaac-Crab-Hex-Student-v0`,
whose `__post_init__` mirrors the 2c MDP (teacher terrain generator at 0.08 m / 40 columns, flat-walk
terminations, `full` actions, DR on the chassis, the same knobs, no reward anneal) while its scene and
observation configs add the depth camera.

### 2.2 Observations

The policy observation is one term, `extreme_parkour_observations`, computed by
`mdp/observations.py::CrabHexParkourObservations`. Its proprioceptive block is 89 dims on the 24-joint plant,
in this order: body angular velocity ×0.25 (3), roll and pitch (2), steering channels [0, Δyaw, Δnext-yaw]
(3), command [0, 0, vx] (3), env index and its inverse (2), planar body velocity ×2 (2), gait-clock sin and
cos (2), joint positions minus defaults (24; the cam-shaft channels wrapped to ±π), joint velocities ×0.05
(24), the last clipped action (18), and six per-leg *contact proxies* (6). The contact proxy is a
motor-current model, `max(current_hip, current_knee) − 0.5` per leg with a rod-speed gate, because the
hardware has no foot sensors; rewards and terminations keep using the simulator's contact sensor. To that
block the parkour stack appends the 108-point height scan (a 2.4 × 3.4 m grid at 0.3 m, 9 × 12 rays, biased
0.6 m ahead; `crab_hex_scene_cfg.py::_apply_crab_height_scanner`), the 9 privileged explicit dims (body
velocity) and the 53 privileged latents (mass 1, CoM 3, friction 1, 24 joint-stiffness and 24 damping ratios),
and the actor keeps a history of 10 frames: 89 × 11 + 108 + 9 + 53 = 1149 dims, the `obs_dim_actual` every eval
records. The network slices that vector with `num_prop = 75`, `num_scan = 132`, `num_priv_latent = 41`
(`agents/crab_hex_rl_cfg.py`, sizes that predate the 24-joint plant and the clock dims); train and play share
the slicing, so it is self-consistent, but the "scan" slice is not the height scan
(`docs/crab-hex-forward-policy-config.md`, "Observation and action"). The student adds the
`depth_camera` group: a 58×87 ray-cast depth image from a camera 0.37 m ahead and 0.10 m above the chassis
origin, buffered two frames, plus a `delta_yaw_ok` flag.

Two facts about the observation matter for using the heads. The steering channels are zero on flat tiles
*unless* `KRABBY_HEADING` is set, in which case they carry the heading-command error — heads from `2b` on
were trained with it live. `KRABBY_PHASE` exports the band for training and play, but the evals of record did
not: the flat canary and step onset (`run_phases.py::eval_morph`, and the recipe in `README.md`) export only
the five canary keys, so those numbers were scored with the steering channels zeroed; only the obstacle course
(`eval_obst`, via `run_exposure.CANARY_KEEP`) carries `KRABBY_HEADING=-1.2:1.2` for 2b+ heads. Re-scoring a
head by hand must copy whichever convention the number being compared used ([§7.1](#71-scenarios)). And the
gait clock the policy observes is the same buffer the clock rewards pay against
([§6.3](#63-gait-clock)).

### 2.3 Actions

18 channels through `mdp/parkour_actions.py::CrabHexDelayedJointPositionAction`: six cam-shaft *velocity*
targets (scale π rad/s = the 30 RPM gearmotor at full command) and twelve hip-femur / femur-tibia
*position* targets (scale 0.25 rad). The six body-hip yaw joints are passive: their targets are computed
from the cam-shaft angle each substep (the mechanical cam mapping). Raw policy outputs are clipped to ±1 by
the vec-env wrapper (`ParkourRslRlVecEnvWrapper`, configured by the runner cfg's `clip_actions = 1.0`), the position channels are additionally clipped to ±4.8 by the action term
(the legacy bridge stages used ±1 there), a one-step delay and an 8-frame history are applied, and the
hip / knee targets then pass through the linear-actuator layer (rod-length window and slew limits), and the
cam-speed command is acceleration-slewed at π/0.5 rad/s² — zero to full speed in 0.5 s, as the gearmotor ramps
rather than steps (`mdp/crab_hex_dimensions.py::CAM_ACCEL_LIMIT_RAD_S2`; it removed the instant-full-throttle
wheelie of the Phase-0 smoke tests). The
runner clip is load-bearing: the first 3a run, launched without it, drove joints at ~5× the trained
authority and never left the falling regime ([§8](#8-branches-not-baked-and-roads-not-taken)).

### 2.4 Terminations and episode

`crab_failure` (`config/crab_hex/crab_hex_mdp_terminations.py::terminate_crab_hex_failure`, with the thresholds
set by `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py::CrabHexFlatWalkTerminationsCfg`) ends an episode when the body tilts more
than 0.5 rad from upright or a hip link sees more than 500 N of contact force (the sensor is restricted to
`.*_Hip`; the teacher cfg's `hip_contact_sensor_cfg` comment records that chassis contacts were dropped because
benign brushes were ending episodes, and the hip check exists so a collapsed hip-on-ground posture ends early
instead of burning the horizon). `total_terminates` is the time-out term (40 s,
also roll / pitch beyond 1.5 rad, the root below −0.25 m, or all goals reached) and is bootstrapped as a
truncation. No preset overrides either threshold (`KRABBY_TERM_ANGLE` / `KRABBY_TERM_CONTACT_N` unused).

### 2.5 How rewards are accounted

`parkour/parkour_isaaclab/managers/parkour_reward_manager.py` sums `func × weight × dt` over the registered
terms each step, **skips any term whose weight is exactly 0** (its `Episode_Reward/<term>` curve is still logged but flatlines at 0),
and **clips the per-step total at 0**. Two consequences shape the catalogue in [§4](#4-reward-catalogue):
penalties can only reduce the positive income of the same step (a falling policy cannot escape a penalty by
dying early, which is why several shaping terms are written as a bonus rather than a penalty), and a
phased-out term is ramped to eps = 1e-3 rather than 0 so its telemetry keeps flowing (`mdp/curriculums.py`).
Every weight knob is presence-based: an explicitly exported `KRABBY_<TERM>_W=0` disables a baked default.
Foot order everywhere is FL, FR, ML, MR, RL, RR; the tripod sets are A = {FL, MR, RL} and B = {FR, ML, RR}.

<!-- /generated:phase-head-1a -->

**Goal.** Form the alternating-tripod gait from scratch (no checkpoint is resumed), with the scaffolding at
full strength, on the A15+B plant — whose only prior training was the morphology × exposure campaign's P1 / P2
arms, one of which this window reproduces. The gait is *specified* rather than discovered: the clock-schedule
term pays for feet that are unloaded when their clock window says swing and planted-and-still when it says
stance, and the swing-apex term pays for reaching 8 cm at mid-swing. The clock schedule is what first produced a
real tripod on the measured plant (`experiments/2026-08-22_1200_gait_formation_v2/CHANGELOG.md`, E1 row and the
BAKE row of 2026-08-23 — clock 1.0, σ² 0.1, L1 −1.0, band 0.0–0.35, mirror loss and RSI 0.2, without apex; its
E1 / E3b rows: clock alone formed the tripod on one seed of four (E1 seed 1; seeds 2–4 crept), and clock plus a
high-quality RSI bank made the "unflippable" seed 2 walk (E3b)); the apex term was added in Phase 4 for lift and
baked alongside light terrain in the M1C final bake. Reference-state initialisation seeds 20 % of resets from frames of the
target gait so the schedule income is live from iteration 0 and the problem is retention rather than
exploration (`mdp/crab_hex_rsi.py` docstring). The mirror-symmetry loss stays on (it broke the tripod
plateau that five reward versions never reached, `agents/rsl_rl_ppo_cfg.py::_apply_flat_walk_symmetry`).
Two levers make the policy actually train *walking*: walking slots (`KRABBY_STAND_FRAC=0.2`: a command slot
stands with probability 0.2, otherwise commands 0.21–0.35 m/s, replacing the stock rule that zeroed ~57 %
of draws on the 0.0–0.35 band) and 40 s episodes made of four 10 s holds, so training sees the sustained
holds the evals score (`experiments/2026-09-04_1105_morph_x_exposure/CHANGELOG.md`, Question and the
countervailing golden-plant evidence). Terrain is *light*: 80 % flat tiles with shallow obstacles at
difficulty 0.05–0.2 mixed in and levels frozen, so lifting is learned while the gait is still plastic
(`crab_hex_env_cfg.py`, the NOTE at `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_MODE`: obstacles-after-consolidation plateaued in
the carry-up lineages). Tracking pressure is the widened exponential well (σ² = 0.1) plus the constant-
gradient L1 penalty at −1.0, so the walk cannot settle at a fixed speed deficit.

## 4. Reward catalogue

### 4.0 Common rules and gates

All terms are functions in `parkour/parkour_isaaclab/envs/mdp/rewards.py` unless a crab-specific module is
named (the two `track_*_exp` terms are Isaac Lab stock); each returns a non-negative magnitude by convention
and the sign lives in the weight (the one exception, `reward_tracking_goal_vel`, is noted). Shared conventions:
"contact" for the footfall penalties means the footpad's net contact force exceeds 0.1 N
(`contact_force_threshold`); the touchdown / lift-off events and air / contact times used by
`reward_feet_air_time_positive` and `reward_stride_length` come from the contact sensor's own bookkeeping, which
switches at its `force_threshold` = 1.0 N (`crab_hex_scene_cfg.py`); the clock terms use smooth force kernels
instead of a threshold; "forward commanded" means the commanded
vx exceeds `min_cmd_norm` = 0.12 m/s; "upright" means the body z-axis projection exceeds 0.9; "obstacle
tiles only" means the term is multiplied by (1 − flat-tile mask) of `base_parkour`; the clock terms use the
gait clock of [§6.3](#63-gait-clock). Weights per phase are in [§4.10](#410-term--phase-weight-matrix) and
telemetry appears as `Episode_Reward/<term>`. Long-form derivations and design histories live in the
module docstrings of `mdp/crab_hex_clock_reward.py`, `mdp/crab_hex_stride_reward.py` and
`mdp/crab_hex_tripod_reward.py`, and in `experiments/lit-review-hexapod-reward-stability.md`.

### 4.1 Command tracking

#### `track_lin_vel_xy_exp`
- **Meaning.** The main positive income for matching the commanded planar velocity.
- **Formula.** `exp(-|cmd_xy - v_xy|^2 / std^2)` (Isaac Lab stock term).
- **Params.** `std` = √0.1 ≈ 0.316 in every phase (`KRABBY_TRACK_SIGMA2=0.1`; the class default √0.02 gives
  a ±0.25 m/s capture radius outside which the gradient is dead — `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py`, the
  tracking-regression NOTE of 2026-08-15: under velocity-action exploration the narrow well's gradient was zero
  forever; root cause in `experiments/2026-08-13_2000_onedir_spin`).
- **Weight.** +1.25 in every RL phase. Knob: parameter only (`KRABBY_TRACK_SIGMA2`).

#### `track_ang_vel_z_exp`
- **Meaning.** Income for matching the commanded yaw rate — always the heading P-controller's output
  ([§6.1](#61-commands-walking-slots-and-heading)): a heading-hold toward yaw 0 in 1a / 2a, and from 2b the
  tracking of heading targets drawn from ±1.2 rad.
- **Formula.** `exp(-(cmd_wz - w_z)^2 / 0.25)` (stock term, `std` = 0.5).
- **Weight.** +1.0 in every RL phase.

#### `penalty_tracking_error_l1`
- **Meaning.** Constant-gradient pressure toward exact tracking: unlike the exponential well it cannot be
  satisfied at a fixed deficit.
- **Formula.** `|cmd_xy - v_xy|_2` (linear in the error magnitude).
- **Weight.** −1.0 in every RL phase (`KRABBY_TRACK_L1_W`; class default −0.5).
- **Why.** Baked as task1-velocity C1 on 2026-08-16, the change that restored command-following in the velocity
  era (deficits ≤ 0.08 vs the era's 0.25–0.48 command-blindness; `experiments/2026-08-15_1530_task1_velocity`,
  `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py` NOTE).

#### `reward_forward_progress_along_command`
- **Meaning.** Dense income for body velocity along the commanded direction, capped so a speed exploit
  cannot pay.
- **Formula.** `clamp(v_xy . cmd_dir, 0, max_speed_scale)` when forward commanded.
- **Params.** `max_speed_scale` 1.05 (`KRABBY_MAX_SPEED_SCALE`; the previous config value 1.75 paid a wheelie
  exploit up to 1.14 m/s — gait-formation Phase 0, 2026-08-20: both smoke tests rode it into the 0.5 rad tilt
  termination, `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py` NOTE on `max_speed_scale`; the function default is 2.0).
- **Weight.** +0.6 in every RL phase.

#### `penalty_lin_vel_y`
- **Meaning.** Penalises sideways body velocity when no lateral motion is commanded and the robot is
  walking forward.
- **Formula.** `v_y^2 * [|cmd_y| < 0.05] * [|cmd_x| > 0.12]`.
- **Weight.** −3.0 in every RL phase.

### 4.2 Posture and base motion

#### `reward_orientation`
- **Meaning.** Tilt penalty (roll and pitch through the projected gravity vector), on flat tiles only —
  obstacle tiles legitimately tilt the body.
- **Formula.** `|g_b,xy|^2`, zeroed on non-flat tiles.
- **Weight.** −0.7 in every RL phase (`KRABBY_ORIENT_W`).

#### `reward_lin_vel_z`
- **Meaning.** Vertical body-velocity penalty (bouncing), halved on obstacle tiles.
- **Formula.** `v_z^2`, ×0.5 where the tile is not flat.
- **Weight.** −0.15 in every RL phase.

### 4.3 Gait-formation income (the scaffold, phased out over 2a–2c)

#### `reward_clock_schedule`
- **Meaning.** Pays each foot for obeying the clock: unloaded during its swing window, planted *and still*
  during its stance window. It is the term that specifies the tripod (set B's offset is π), and the one
  scaffold kept at half strength in the shipped teacher.
- **Formula.** Per foot, with `phi_i = clock + offset_i`: `I_swing = sigmoid(sin(phi_i) / 0.15)`,
  `I_stance = 1 - I_swing`, `swing_ok = exp(-(F_i / 100 N)^2)`,
  `stance_ok = (1 - exp(-(F_i / 50 N)^2)) * exp(-(|v_i,xy| / 0.10 m/s)^2)`; income = mean over feet of
  `I_swing * swing_ok + I_stance * stance_ok` (`combine = "sum"`), times the upright gate. On a stop
  command every window is stance. Income lies in [0, 1] and no holdable state earns full income while the
  clock advances (`mdp/crab_hex_clock_reward.py::clock_schedule_income`, wrapper
  `rewards.py::RewardClockContactSchedule`).
- **Params.** `combine` sum (the product form would zero a creeper's free ~0.5), `min_upright_gz` 0.9.
- **Weight.** +1.0 in 1a, 2a, 2b; 1.0 → 0.5 during 2c (`KRABBY_CLOCK_W`, `KRABBY_PHASEOUT`); held at 0.5
  thereafter by decision ([§3.4](#34-phase-2c--clock-anneal-to-05-window-3-2c_teacher)).
- **Why.** First real tripod on the measured-hardware (legacy golden) plant, before the A15+B rebuild
  (`experiments/2026-08-22_1200_gait_formation_v2/CHANGELOG.md`, E1);
  anneal schedule (`experiments/2026-08-31_1414_gait_income_phaseout/CHANGELOG.md`).

#### `reward_clock_swing_apex`
- **Meaning.** Walk-These-Ways-style *specified* foot-swing height: pays for the foot being near 8 cm
  above the ground plane at the dead centre of its swing window.
- **Formula.** `i_mid = clamp((sin(phi_i) - 0.7) / 0.3, 0, 1)`, `apex_ok = exp(-((h_i - 0.08) / 0.03)^2)`,
  income = `sum(i_mid * apex_ok) / sum(i_mid)`, zero on stops, times the upright gate; `h_i` is the foot
  height above `root_z − 1.05 m` (`clock_swing_apex_income`).
- **Params.** `apex_m` 0.08, `sigma_m` 0.03.
- **Weight.** +1.0 in 1a; 1.0 → 0.5 in 2a; 0.5 → eps in 2b; eps after (`KRABBY_APEX_W`).
- **Why.** Income-priced clearance was lost to survival economics at every dose, so the apex is specified
  in the schedule instead (config NOTE at the term; gait_formation_v2 Phase 4).

#### `reward_feet_air_time_positive`
- **Meaning.** Bonus at each touchdown proportional to how much longer than a threshold the foot was in
  the air — rewards real swings, never penalises short ones.
- **Formula.** `sum_feet relu(air_time - 0.38 s) * first_contact`, gated on `|cmd_xy| > 0.1`.
- **Params.** `threshold` 0.38 s (cam-derived: the return stroke at π rad/s lasts ≈ 0.68 s;
  `KRABBY_AIRTIME_THRESH`).
- **Weight.** class 0.8 in 1a; 0.8 → 0.4 in 2a; 0.4 → eps in 2b; eps after (`KRABBY_AIRTIME_W`).
- **Why.** 0.4 → 0.8 was the short-run campaign's winner (threshold sweeps at 0.10 / 0.15 did not move gait
  quality), and the 0.38 s threshold is the task1-velocity C2 bake of 2026-08-16 rescaled for the π rad/s cam
  (`experiments/2026-08-09_0920_short_runs/CHANGELOG.md`; `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py`, the NOTEs on the term).

#### `reward_stride_length`
- **Meaning.** Pays, at lift-off, the body's forward progress accumulated while that leg was planted,
  raised to a convex power — so one long stance beats several short ones (the anti "tippy-tap" term).
- **Formula.** Per leg: `accum += clamp(v_xy . cmd_dir, 0) * dt` while in contact (reset at first
  contact); at a valid lift-off (`first_air` and stance longer than `min_phase_duration`) pay
  `accum^power` (`mdp/crab_hex_stride_reward.py::stride_length_reward_step`, v3).
- **Params.** `power` 2.0, `min_phase_duration` 0.1 s, `min_cmd_norm` 0.12.
- **Weight.** 0.5 in 1a; 0.5 → 0.25 in 2a; 0.25 → eps in 2b; eps after (`KRABBY_STRIDE_W`).
- **Why.** v2 paid hip-yaw joint displacement over both swing and stance, and its swing half was exploited by a
  ~195 mm foot snap inside a single 20 ms physics step; v3 pays only body progress accumulated during stance
  (module docstring; `experiments/2026-08-08_1701_stride_length_v3`).

### 4.4 Footfall shaping penalties

#### `penalty_tibia_deviation_in_stance`
- **Meaning.** Keeps the knee joints near their default angle while the foot is loaded (stance-gated
  joint shaping; the swing is free).
- **Formula.** `sum_j (q_j - q_default,j)^2 * in_contact_j` over the six femur-tibia joints
  (`rewards.py::penalty_joint_deviation_when_in_contact`).
- **Weight.** −0.28 in every RL phase.

#### `penalty_foot_idle_when_forward`
- **Meaning.** A foot that has not touched down for more than `max_idle_steps` while walking forward is
  penalised per extra step — against a leg that hangs.
- **Formula.** `sum_feet relu(idle_steps - 90)` when forward commanded (`PenaltyFootIdleWhenForward`).
- **Params.** - **Params.** `max_idle_steps` 90 (1.8 s).
- **Weight.** −0.12 in every RL phase.
- **Why.** Stronger doses (−0.25 here, −0.4 on the excess-contact term) were tried in the short-run campaign and
  reverted (two of the three contact-shape arms hit the completion guardrail); at floor gait weights `foot_idle`
  at −0.12 is the load-bearing anti-skate / drag defence (`experiments/2026-08-09_0920_short_runs/CHANGELOG.md`,
  item 4; `experiments/2026-08-31_1414_gait_income_phaseout/CHANGELOG.md`, the floor-weight replay gate). The
  90-step limit replaced 60, which taxed legitimate half-speed cam swings (return stroke ≈ 1.36 s at half speed).

#### `penalty_excess_feet_contact_forward`
- **Meaning.** Penalises having more than four feet on the ground while walking forward — a tripod keeps
  three, a shuffler keeps five or six.
- **Formula.** `relu(n_feet_in_contact - 4)` when forward commanded.
- **Weight.** −0.2 in every RL phase (`KRABBY_EXCESS_CONTACT_W`).
- **Why.** −0.4 was tried in the short-run campaign and reverted (item 4, with the foot-idle dose above).

### 4.5 Smoothness and effort

#### `reward_action_rate`
- **Meaning.** Penalises frame-to-frame changes of the raw action vector.
- **Formula.** `|a_t - a_(t-1)|_2` on the clipped policy output.
- **Weight.** −0.3 in every RL phase (`KRABBY_ACTION_RATE_W`).
- **Why.** The pair replaced the cam-motor direction-reversal penalty: in the short-run campaign the strong
  combination −0.3 / −1e-6 with the reversal penalty off was the best arm of the campaign (tippy-tap 16.6 %,
  stride 0.18 m, completion 100 %), and the 2000-iteration validation cleared every Task-1 target
  (`experiments/2026-08-09_0920_short_runs/CHANGELOG.md`, Arm B; `agents/parkour_mdp_cfg.py`, the
  short-run-campaign NOTE). Training-time tracking error was slightly elevated by them — the trade the phase-out
  campaign later measured as the largest penalty bucket (`action_rate` −0.178 of −0.415 total,
  `experiments/2026-08-31_1414_gait_income_phaseout/CHANGELOG.md`).

#### `reward_delta_torques`
- **Meaning.** Penalises frame-to-frame torque changes over all joints (a small smoothness term).
- **Formula.** `sum (tau_t - tau_(t-1))^2`.
- **Weight.** −1e-6 in every RL phase (`KRABBY_DELTA_TORQUE_W`).
- **Why.** The other half of the short-run smoothness pair (see `reward_action_rate`).

### 4.6 Parkour safety elements (from 2a)

#### `reward_feet_edge`
- **Meaning.** Penalises a loaded foot standing on a terrain edge (rasterised from the generator's edge
  mask), once the terrain level is above 3.
- **Formula.** `[level > 3] * sum_feet (contact and at_edge)` with contact meaning > 2 N in the current or the
  oldest frame of the sensor's force history.
- **Weight.** −0.3 from 2a (`KRABBY_EDGE_W`).

#### `reward_feet_stumble`
- **Meaning.** Flags a foot pushing sideways harder than four times its vertical load — it stubbed
  something.
- **Formula.** `any_feet(|F_xy| > 4 |F_z|)` (binary per env).
- **Weight.** −1.0 from 2a (`KRABBY_STUMBLE_W`).

#### `reward_collision`
- **Meaning.** Counts chassis, hip and femur links in contact (> 0.1 N); hips are included so the policy
  cannot minimise torque by sitting on them.
- **Formula.** number of listed bodies with contact.
- **Weight.** −2.0 from 2a (`KRABBY_COLLISION_W`).

### 4.7 Goal and heading elements on obstacle tiles

#### `reward_tracking_yaw`
- **Meaning.** Income for facing the next parkour goal, on obstacle tiles only (the flat stack binds the
  masked `reward_tracking_yaw_on_parkour` function under this name).
- **Formula.** `(1 - flat) * exp(-|target_yaw - yaw|) * [cmd_x > 0.12]`.
- **Weight.** +0.2 from 2a (`KRABBY_YAW_W`).

#### `reward_tracking_goal_vel`
- **Meaning.** Velocity toward the current parkour goal relative to the commanded speed, on obstacle
  tiles only; the one term that can go negative (moving away from the goal), clamped to ±10.
- **Formula.** `(1 - flat) * min(v_w,xy . goal_dir, cmd_vx) / max(|cmd_vx|, 0.05)` (sign-preserving floor on
  the divisor), clamped to ±10 (`reward_tracking_goal_vel_on_parkour`).
- **Weight.** +0.75 from 2b (`KRABBY_GOAL_VEL_W`).

### 4.8 Clearance (from 2b)

#### `reward_obstacle_clearance`
- **Meaning.** A sparse bonus for actually crossing: five gates that must all hold — an obstacle tile, no
  stumble, progress toward the goal, forward speed while forward is commanded, and an upright body.
- **Formula.** `(1 - flat) * (1 - stumble) * [goal_prog >= 0.05] * [v_x >= 0.10 and cmd_x > 0.12] * [|g_b,xy|^2 <= 0.02]`.
- **Params.** The flat stack uses the relaxed bootstrap gates `min_goal_progress` 0.05 and
  `min_forward_speed` 0.10 (the legacy 2b2 class used 0.15 / 0.25).
- **Weight.** +0.9 from 2b (`KRABBY_CLEARANCE_W`).

#### `reward_foot_clearance`
- **Meaning.** Dense income for lifting swinging feet above a minimum height, on every tile once the flat
  mask is lifted.
- **Formula.** `mean_feet(in_swing * clamp(relu(h - min_clearance_m), max=0.26))` when forward commanded;
  `h` = foot height above `root_z − 1.05 m`.
- **Params.** `min_clearance_m` 0.03 (class 0.07 → `KRABBY_FOOT_CLEAR_MIN`, the dead-zone fix),
  `max_clearance_m` 0.26, mask lifted (`KRABBY_FOOT_CLEAR_FLAT=1`).
- **Weight.** +1.0 from 2b (`KRABBY_FOOT_CLEAR_W`).

#### `penalty_swing_min_clearance`
- **Meaning.** Penalises micro-swings: a swinging foot lower than 4 cm.
- **Formula.** `mean_feet(in_swing * [h < 0.04])`, same gating and mask as the term above.
- **Weight.** −0.4 from 2b (`KRABBY_SWING_MIN_CLEAR_W`).

Each tile is a 16 × 4 m lane with a 2.5 m start platform and eight goals down its centre line; the

### 5.2 Sub-terrain types

| Sub-terrain | What it is | Parameters the presets touch |
|---|---|---|
| `parkour_flat` | a flat lane with surface roughness only (a hurdle terrain with `apply_flat`) | proportion, `noise_range` |
| `parkour_gap` | trenches across the lane with side trenches, leaving a walk-through corridor of `half_valid_width` | `gap_depth`, `gap_size`, `half_valid_width`, `y_range` |
| `parkour_hurdle` | raised bars that span only the corridor — outside `half_valid_width` the bar is cut back to ground level, so feet beside the corridor meet no hurdle | `hurdle_height_range`, `half_valid_width`, `y_range` |
| `parkour_step` | up / down steps | `step_height`, `half_valid_width`, `y_range` |

| `parkour_demo` | a fixed showcase course independent of difficulty: one 0.30–0.36 m hurdle, two 0.35–0.45 m blocks, a 0.5–0.8 m gap, two more blocks at the same height and two inclined stones offset left / right (`extreme_parkour_terrians.py::parkour_demo_terrain`) — far above this plant's lift | proportion 0 in every preset |

pitch between obstacles is drawn per obstacle from the stock `x_range` — 0.8–1.5 m plus the gap width for gaps,

down), and stones `0.9 - 0.3*difficulty` … `1 - 0.2*difficulty` long separated by `-0.1 … 0.1+0.3*difficulty` of
pit, the last stone 1.6 m — with a 0.1 m flat pad at every tile border (`terrains/extreme_parkour/config/parkour.py`,
`extreme_parkour_terrains_cfg.py`). The eight goals are the start marker, one goal per obstacle (the midpoint
before each gap / hurdle, the top of each step / stone) and the final marker (`parkours/exposure_stats.py`, header).

The crab's stance is 2.38 m wide (half-stance 1.19 m, `mdp/exposure_knobs.py::CRAB_HALF_STANCE_M`), so a
corridor whose half-width is below that puts the outer feet outside the corridor: into the side trench on gap
tiles and the pit on stepping-stone tiles, onto the ground beside the raised corridor on step tiles, and past the
ends of the bars on hurdle tiles — the finding behind the `recal2b2w` widening.

### 5.3 The knob pipeline

`crab_hex_env_cfg.py::apply_flat_walk_knobs` first sets the promotion fractions (`KRABBY_TERRAIN_PROMOTE`,
`up:down`, on the parkour term rather than the terrain generator) and then builds the terrain in this order, so
later terrain knobs override earlier ones:

1. reset to **100 % flat**, curriculum off, difficulty (0.1, 0.25);
2. `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_MODE=light`: freeze the terrain levels, set the flat proportion to
   `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_FLAT_FRAC` (default 0.8) with the remainder split evenly over gap / hurdle / step /
   stones, difficulty band `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_DIFF` (default 0.05:0.2), roughness noise pinned to 0.02 m;
3. (only under `light`) `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_GEOM`: `shallow` (the default, also when unset), `recal2b2` or `recal2b2w`;
4. (only under `light`) `KRABBY_FLAT_TERRAIN_CURRICULUM=1`: re-enable the curriculum and unfreeze the levels;
5. `KRABBY_CORRIDOR_HALF_WIDTH` / `KRABBY_STONE_WIDTH`: post-preset overrides (unused by the lineage).

### 5.5 Curriculum, levels and promotion

With the curriculum on, each **column** of the grid is one sub-terrain type (allocated by proportion) and
each **row** is a difficulty level: `difficulty = lo + (hi − lo) × row / (rows − 1)`, so the 0.20–0.70 band
over 10 rows is 0.20, 0.256, … 0.70 (`parkour_terrain_generator.py`). With the curriculum off (phase 1a),
every tile draws its sub-terrain by proportion and a uniform difficulty in the band, and there are no
levels to climb. Environments start at most at level 2 (`max_init_terrain_level` = 2 on the stock terrain
importer, `parkour_tasks/default_cfg.py`; the play cfgs lift the cap). At each reset the
parkour manager compares the distance walked with the distance the command would have covered:
`move up if distance > up × cmd_vx × episode_s`, `move down if distance < down × cmd_vx × episode_s`
(`parkour/parkour_isaaclab/envs/mdp/parkours/parkour_event.py`); solvers of the top level are re-rolled to
a random level. The stock 0.8 / 0.4 fractions assume a plant that tracks its command; the crab tracks ~0.5,
so the campaigns used 0.45 / 0.25, and the 40 s horizon halves them again to **0.225 / 0.125**
(`crab_hex_phases.py::promote_fracs_for_horizon`). Telemetry: `Metrics/base_parkour/terrain_levels` and
the exposure ledger ([§6.9](#69-exposure-telemetry)). In training the level settled at ~6 of 10.

### 5.6 Spawn placement

Platform spawns are placed by `reset_root_state` at `size_y + offset` = 4.0 + 3.0 = 7.0 m before the tile origin
(the tile centre), i.e. tile-local x = 1.0 m, with the default `offset` of 3.0 (`KRABBY_SPAWN_OFFSET` is unset in
every preset); the downrange spread of PLAN H (`KRABBY_SPAWN_SPREAD`) was not used by the lineage. RSI resets are the
exception: because `KRABBY_RSI_SPAWN_FIX` is unset they land at the **tile centre**, ~7 m downrange on
whatever obstacle sits there, with the flat-harvested height — which is why every window block of the lineage
record shows RSI episodes at `reach_edge 1.000 | reach_obst 1.000 | goals_passed 0.000`
(`experiments/2026-09-06_2130_a15b_lineage/REPORT.md`; the placement was diagnosed in
`experiments/2026-09-03_1156_obstacle_exposure/CHANGELOG.md`, item 11).

## 6. Non-reward, non-terrain configuration

### 6.1 Commands, walking slots and heading

The velocity command is resampled every 10 s from the band `KRABBY_LIN_VEL_X=0.0:0.35` m/s. Without
walking slots the stock rule zeroes any draw below the 0.2 m/s clip — about 57 % of slots on this band,
which is "the robot stands most of the episode". With `KRABBY_STAND_FRAC=0.2` a slot stands with
probability 0.2 and otherwise draws vx uniformly in 0.21–0.35 m/s (the 0.01 margin keeps the gait clock
running) (`parkour/parkour_isaaclab/envs/mdp/parkour_commands/command_sampling.py`). The realised standing
fraction is logged as `Metrics/base_velocity/stand_frac_actual`. From 2b the heading target is drawn from
±1.2 rad and turned into a yaw-rate command by a P-controller with stiffness 1.5, clipped to ±1 rad/s and
zeroed below the 0.4 rad/s clip (`uniform_parkour_command.py`); setting `KRABBY_HEADING` also switches the
flat-tile steering observation from zero to the heading error ([§2.2](#22-observations)). Goal-directed
velocity on obstacle tiles is a reward channel ([§4.7](#47-goal-and-heading-elements-on-obstacle-tiles)),
driven by the parkour manager's eight goals per tile — a start marker, six obstacle goals and a final marker — of
which the current and next are observed (`num_future_goal_obs` = 2); a goal counts as reached once the body has
been within 0.2 m of it for 0.1 s (`reach_goal_delay`), and `Metrics/base_parkour/current_goal_idx` is that index
(`parkours/parkour_event.py::_update_command`, `parkour_events_cfg.py`).

### 6.2 Episode and horizon

Episodes are 40 s (`KRABBY_EPISODE_S`; the stock cfg is 20 s) at 50 Hz control, i.e. four 10 s command
holds, which is the hold length the evals score. The promotion fractions are rescaled by the same factor
([§5.5](#55-curriculum-levels-and-promotion)). Play configs use 60 s.

### 6.3 Gait clock

The clock lives in the action term (`mdp/parkour_actions.py`), advances once per env step before physics,
and is read by both the clock rewards and the observation (sin / cos), so what is paid for and what is
seen are one object. Its cadence is linear in the commanded speed and frozen on stop commands:
`freq_rev_s = clamp(|vx| / 0.35, max 1) × 0.5` when `|vx| > 0.2`, else 0 (`mdp/crab_hex_dimensions.py`:
`CLOCK_F_MAX_REV_S` 0.5, `CLOCK_V_MAX_M_S` 0.35, `CLOCK_CMD_STOP_M_S` 0.2). Full command therefore maps to the
cam motor's full speed (π rad/s = 0.5 rev/s, one 2 s gait cycle). At reset each env draws a random phase
(decorrelates the batch and makes the policy phase-invariant) unless an RSI frame staged one.

### 6.4 Reference-state initialisation (RSI)

`mdp/crab_hex_rsi.py::reset_from_reference_states`: at reset, a Bernoulli(`fraction` = 0.2) draw picks the
envs that start from a random frame of the bank instead of the default stand. A bank holds six arrays per
frame — `joint_pos`, `joint_vel`, `root_quat_w`, `root_z`, `root_lin_vel_b`, `clock_phase` — and the chosen
frame's full joint state, root height, orientation and body-frame velocity (rotated to world) are written
with the env's own spawn x / y, angular velocity zeroed, and the matching gait-clock phase staged on the
action term. The purpose is retention rather than exploration: schedule- and phase-conditioned income is
live from iteration 0. The bank of record is `rsi_bank_P0_null.npz` (2 051 frames), harvested offline by
`experiments/2026-08-22_1200_gait_formation_v2/harvest_rsi_bank.py` from rollouts of the PLAN F gated-lineage
P0_null rung checkpoint (`crab_hex_flat_walk/2026-08-26_21-41-53/model_26899.pt`, the M1C head plus 2k null
iterations) on the flat-walk play task under 0.25–0.35 m/s commands, keeping upright frames moving faster than
0.15 m/s (a policy-generated walk — not motion capture, and not the Phase-0 scripted setAB gait, whose 165-frame
bank is `rsi_bank_setAB.npz`; adopted in `experiments/2026-08-26_2200_gated_lineage/CHANGELOG.md`, the FIX row)
on the **legacy golden** plant; it is reused on A15+B by design, since joint space and root state do not encode the mount
transform (toe height is invariant to it, foot placements are rotated). The presets read it from
`experiments/2026-08-26_2200_gated_lineage/`; `policy/rsi/` holds a byte-identical copy
(`tests/unit/test_policy_of_record.py::test_rsi_bank_asset_is_the_bank_the_presets_use`). RSI spawns land
at the tile centre ([§5.6](#56-spawn-placement)); every RSI spawn is tagged in the exposure telemetry.
The legacy-golden presets use the same P0_null bank for windows 0–1 and the per-window re-harvested banks
`rsi_bank_pg_r1..r4.npz` (from the gait-income phase-out campaign) for windows 2–5.

### 6.5 Domain randomisation

Three events, each removed entirely when its knob is unset (phase 1a) and armed from 2a on
(`crab_hex_env_cfg.py`, the `KRABBY_DR_*` block): `push_by_setting_velocity` (every 8 s of global time the
chassis' planar velocity is overwritten with a uniform draw in ±0.5 m/s on x and y), `randomize_rigid_body_mass` (−0.5 to +1.5 kg added to the
chassis at start-up; the Go2 default was −1 to +3) and `randomize_rigid_body_com` (±0.01 m on each axis;
Go2 ±0.02). Independent of the knobs and on in every phase: friction randomisation over (0.6, 2.0) in 64
buckets at start-up, joint-position scaling (0.95, 1.05) at reset, and the (inert, zero-range) external
force / torque event. The student's `random_camera_position` (pitch ±5°) is the only event unique to 3a.

### 6.6 Mirror-symmetry loss

Every RL phase trains with the left / right mirror loss on: `agents/rsl_rl_ppo_cfg.py::_apply_flat_walk_symmetry`
attaches `RslRlSymmetryCfg(use_mirror_loss=True, use_data_augmentation=False)` with coefficient
`KRABBY_SYM_LOSS_COEF` (default 0.5) and `mdp/crab_hex_mirror.py::crab_hex_symmetry_augmentation`, which
swaps FL↔FR, ML↔MR, RL↔RR, flips the lateral channels, and advances the clock by π (the mirrored gait is
the same schedule half a cycle later). Because the tripod sets map onto each other, a mirror-equivariant
policy cannot prefer a lead set. The 20k validation run broke the tripod plateau (0.517 vs 0.401) with no
degradation (`experiments/2026-08-13_0035_mirror_symmetry`). Distillation has no symmetry loss.

### 6.7 Reward-weight phase-out (`KRABBY_PHASEOUT`)

The gait-income anneal is a curriculum term, not a reward-config change: `apply_flat_walk_knobs` reads
`KRABBY_PHASEOUT` (`term:w0:w1:t0:t1[,…]`) and attaches one `ramp_reward_weight` curriculum term per entry
(`mdp/curriculums.py::phaseout_curriculum_cfg_from_env`), which each step sets the named reward term's weight to a
cosine from `w0` to `w1` over env steps `[t0, t1]` (`w0` before, `w1` after, `w1 + (w0 − w1)·½(1 + cos(π·frac))`
between) and writes it back through `reward_manager.set_term_cfg`, so the logged curriculum value is the live
weight. Env steps count from process start (`env.common_step_counter`), which is why every preset uses
`t0:t1` = `0:24000` (1 000 iterations × 24 steps per env per iteration) and why a ramp restarts at every resume
([§9](#9-provenance-and-caveats)). Targets are clamped to |w| ≥ 1e-3 so a phased-out term is never parked at
exactly 0 (the manager would skip it and its `Episode_Reward/` telemetry — the campaigns' gait gate — would
flatline). The presets derive the spec from `crab_hex_phases.py::GAIT_RAMPS` via `phaseout_spec`: 2a ramps apex
1.0 → 0.5, air time 0.8 → 0.4, stride 0.5 → 0.25; 2b ramps the three to 0.001; 2c ramps the clock schedule
1.0 → 0.5; 1a has none and the student MDP drops it (`include_reward_anneal=False`). The window-start weights
themselves are the `KRABBY_<TERM>_W` values of `GAIT_WEIGHTS`. A malformed spec raises at launch.

### 6.8 PPO and distillation

The RL phases use `PPOWithExtractor` through the crab runner (`OnPolicyRunnerCrabHex`) with the same
optimiser settings in 1a and 2a–2c (the phase branch of the teacher runner sets the flat-walk learning rate
and noise): learning rate 3e-4 with the adaptive schedule (desired KL 0.01), entropy 0.01, clip 0.2, 5
epochs × 16 minibatches, 24 steps per env, γ 0.99, λ 0.95, gradient norm 1.0, initial action noise 1.5,
runner `clip_actions` 1.0, RMA DAgger updates every 20 iterations with the privileged-regularisation
schedule [0, 0.1, 2000, 3000]. Networks: `CrabHexActorCriticRMA` with actor / critic [512, 256, 128], scan
encoder [128, 64, 32], privileged encoder [64, 20], ELU, a 10-frame state-history encoder, and a
`DefaultEstimator` [128, 64] (lr 1e-4) trained on estimated states. The paradigm names *pure student* (phase 1) and
*teacher–student* (phase 2) refer to this machinery, not to the depth student: `CrabHexActorCriticRMA` has two
routes to one latent — a privileged encoder reading the privileged-latent slice and a `StateHistoryEncoder`
reading the state-history slice (`modules/actor_critic_with_encoder.py`, `infer_priv_latent` /
`infer_hist_latent`; the slice sizes are the constants of [§2.2](#22-observations)). PPO trains the privileged
route and adds a regulariser pulling the privileged latent toward the history latent, coefficient ramping
0 → 0.1 between algorithm updates 2000 and 5000 of each launch — the counter advances on every PPO update and on every DAgger update, so roughly iterations 1 900–4 760 (`priv_reg_coef_schedual = [0, 0.1, 2000, 3000]`,
`modules/ppo_with_extractor.py`; the counter is not saved with the checkpoint); every `dagger_update_freq` = 20
iterations the rollout uses the history route (`hist_encoding = it % 20 == 0`) and `update_dagger` fits the
history encoder to the privileged latent — the RMA adaptation module, trained DAgger-style on the policy's own
rollouts. The `DefaultEstimator` predicts the 9 explicit privileged dims from the proprioceptive block, and
`train_with_estimated_states = True` means the actor acts on that estimate. Every head therefore already
contains a proprioception-only route; phase 3 acts through it with the depth latent in place of the
height-scan latent ([§3.5](#35-phase-3a--depth-student-distillation-3a_student-the-shipped-head)). Distillation (3a) uses
`DistillationWithExtractor`: learning rate 2e-4, 4 minibatches, initial noise 0.65, `clip_actions` 1.0,
and the depth encoder (`DepthOnlyFCBackbone58x87` + `RecurrentDepthBackbone`, hidden 512, lr 1e-3, 120-step
rollouts). Every phase ran 5 000 iterations at seed 3 with 256 (RL) or 192 (distillation) environments;
a distillation phase took 9.87 h of wall time (7.2 s per iteration at 192 envs, ≈ 13.7 GB of GPU memory with
the teacher terrain generator under the depth ray-caster; `experiments/2026-09-07_1330_phase_pipeline/REPORT.md`,
the 3a / 3b blocks, and `CHARTER.md`, "Smoke 3a"), an RL window ≈ 2.4 h including its three evals
(`experiments/2026-09-06_2130_a15b_lineage/CHANGELOG.md`, ledger timestamps; ≈ 20 h per six-window seed). The
per-phase settings are in the tables of [§3](#3-training-phases).

### 6.9 Exposure telemetry

Always on, independent of the rewards: `parkour/parkour_isaaclab/envs/mdp/parkours/exposure_stats.py` keeps,
per metric, a ring of the last 1 024 finished episodes in that metric's group and emits `Metrics/base_parkour/reach_edge_frac`,
`reach_obst_frac`, `field_frac_mean`, `field_steps_mean`, `goals_passed_mean`, `obst_coverage_1..6` (plus
`_rsi` twins for RSI-spawned episodes), `crab_failure_flat / obst / obst_rsi` and the per-1 000-step hazard
variants, `ep_steps_flat / obst`, `spread_frac_actual` and `rsi_frac_actual`. The same code scores the
offline summaries, so the numbers in the campaign reports are the ones in TensorBoard.

Meanings (`exposure_stats.py`, header): `reach_edge_frac` / `reach_obst_frac` = share of platform-spawned
obstacle-tile episodes whose furthest point passed the platform edge (2.5 m tile-local) / reached the first
obstacle goal; `goals_passed_mean` = obstacle goals passed (the start marker never counts); `field_frac_mean` /
`field_steps_mean` = share / count of steps spent past the edge; `obst_coverage_k` = share of episodes that
reached obstacle k; `crab_failure_flat / obst` = failure share per tile class and `crab_failure_hazard_*` =
failures per 1 000 env steps (horizon-neutral); `*_rsi` = the same over RSI-spawned episodes. Every logged value
is therefore a trailing mean over its group's ring.

### 7.3 Metrics and caveats

*Completion* (`schedule_completion_rate`) is the fraction of the 100 episodes still running when the hold
schedule ends — any termination (fall, timeout or other) counts against it; *falls* counts the `crab_failure`
terminations (almost all forward pitches on this plant; the per-direction split is in each run's
`scenario_metrics.json` under `fall_classes`); the *tripod score* is `0.5 × (coh_A + coh_B) × max(0, −corr(a, b))`
per steady-state window, where `a` / `b` are the per-step stance counts of tripod sets A and B and `coh` is the
fraction of steps on which a set's three feet agree (all loaded or all unloaded) — a clean tripod alternates the
sets (anti-correlated counts), a shuffle or a statue does not; windows shorter than `min_window_s` = 1 s are
discarded, windows with fewer than `min_cycles` = 2 gait cycles are flagged low-confidence, and the tables quote
the median across episodes (`scripts/gait_eval/metrics.py::tripod_window_metrics`, `report.py`). Windows are cut
with the manifest defaults (1.5 s prologue, 1.0 s settle after each command change, 0.5 s excluded before a fall,
contact = footpad force > 1 N). Two caveats: differences of a few
falls in 100 are eval noise (the 3a-vs-2c comparisons are all within it), and the same head can land in one
of two repeatable outcome classes depending on process state ([§9](#9-provenance-and-caveats)).

## 9. Provenance and caveats

- **The 0.79 vs 0.75 flat canary.** The recorded 3a flat number (0.79 / 21) was measured with the
  pre-2026-09-09 morph manifest, whose `slow__A15pB` carried an `env:` block; on the current manifest
  (no block) the same head scores 0.75 / 25. Any environment-variable write after Kit start-up moves the
  depth student between these two repeatable outcomes — same policy, different process state (task README
  §4.1b; `policy/manifest.yaml`, the 3a notes).
- **RSI bank plant mismatch.** The bank was harvested on the legacy golden plant and reused on A15+B by
  design ([§6.4](#64-reference-state-initialisation-rsi)).
- **How the plant was selected.** Windows 0–3 were trained with `KRABBY_HEX_USD_PATH` pointing at
  `assets/variants/crab_simple__splay15_axis2p5in.usda`, which is byte-identical to today's main asset
  `assets/crab.usda`; the presets select the same bytes through `KRABBY_PLANT=A15+B` (nothing exported).
  Records written before 2026-09-09 that say "golden" or "base" mean the *legacy* golden plant.
- **Task names.** Windows 1–3 were trained as `Isaac-Crab-Hex-Flat-Walk-v0` windows by the lineage driver
  while the presets `2a`/`2b`/`2c` name `Isaac-Crab-Hex-Teacher-v0` with a mode; the config-identity test
  proves the two are the same environment (and pins each preset to the window's recorded `env.yaml`).
- **Ramps restart at every resume.** `KRABBY_PHASEOUT` counts env steps from process start, so each
  window's ramp runs over its own first 1 000 iterations regardless of the resumed iteration count.
- **Single seed.** Seed 3 throughout; the seed-2 replay was waived.
- **The observation warning.** The harness prints an `obs_dim_warning` because the actor's `num_prop` /
  `num_scan` slicing (75 / 132) does not match the crab's block sizes; train and play share the slicing,
  so it is self-consistent, but the "scan" slice is not the height scan.

## 10. Reproducing a phase

The phase driver is the route of record (task README §4.0): run from `krabby-research/parkour/` with the
Isaac venv python; `--campaign-dir` must be absolute because the driver runs in a systemd scope whose
working directory is the repo root.

```bash
T="$KRABBY_ROOT/krabby-research/parkour/parkour_tasks/parkour_tasks/crab_hex_forward_task"
"$T/experiments/tools/launch_phases.sh" --campaign-dir "$T/experiments/<YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_name>" \
  --plant A15+B --phases 1a,2a,2b,2c,3a --seed 3            # chains the phases, evals after each, pauses after 2c and 3a
```

By hand, one phase at a time (the preset is the environment; the driver only adds `OMNI_KIT_ACCEPT_EULA=yes` and
strips any inherited `KRABBY_*` variables — do the same, since an exported `KRABBY_*` wins over the preset):

```bash
cd "$KRABBY_ROOT/krabby-research/parkour"; PY="$KRABBY_ROOT/isaac_venv/bin/python"
# the formation phase, from scratch
KRABBY_PHASE=1a $PY scripts/rsl_rl/train.py --task Isaac-Crab-Hex-Flat-Walk-v0 --headless --num_envs 256 --seed 3 \
  --max_iterations 5000
# an RL phase (2b shown), resumed from the previous head
KRABBY_PHASE=2b $PY scripts/rsl_rl/train.py --task Isaac-Crab-Hex-Teacher-v0 --headless --num_envs 256 --seed 3 \
  --max_iterations 5000 --resume --checkpoint "$T/policy/2a_elements/model_9998.pt"
# the distillation phase
KRABBY_PHASE=3a $PY scripts/rsl_rl/train.py --task Isaac-Crab-Hex-Student-v0 --headless --num_envs 192 --seed 3 \
  --max_iterations 5000 --resume --checkpoint "$T/policy/2c_teacher/model_19996.pt"
```

`--checkpoint` must be an absolute path — a relative value is looked up under `logs/rsl_rl/<experiment>/` instead
(task README §4.0).

Evaluating a head with the driver's canary recipe is in [`README.md`](README.md), "Using the heads".
