## Confounds

- **[open] (a) priv_reg ramp 0.038 -> 0.1 coincides with the late formation; C0 under the same ramp did not move**: Timing does not lock to the ramp: the second hand was already rising before the fork at coef 0.021-0.038 (2500->2999: s11 R +0.019, s13 R +0.011, s14 R +0.041, s15 L +0.046, gate/*_m2500_lr.json vs *_lr.json); in the fork the bulk of three rises falls in 3100->4000 (coef 0.042->0.073: s11 +0.077, s13 +0.048, s14 +0.089) but s15's L adds only +0.011 there and +0.031 after 5000 at a flat coef of 0.1, and s11's R then falls -0.020 under the same flat coef. The term is small (priv_reg*coef ~8e-5 vs |surrogate| ~1e-2 at the head, tfevents). The C0 control rules out little: it is one seed in a different regime (median |mu| 6.7/9.8, sat1 ~0.97, no bound loss) whose immobility under the ramp says nothing about whether the ramp helped a non-saturated B policy. No B fork ran with the ramp frozen, so the ramp cannot be separated from extra iterations; the data make it unlikely to be the sole cause but do not exclude a contribution. Also CHARTER 10.5's 'made at coefficient 0' is factually wrong (0.0383 at 2999, nonzero since it 1905).
- **[weakens] (b) env restart at the fork (fresh synchronized episodes, empty rollout buffer) rather than the extra iterations**: The model_3100 reads show no jump at the restart: second hands s11 -0.005, s13 +0.011, s14 +0.017, s15 -0.001, and s12's hands -0.009/+0.031 (x6k/gate/*_m3100_lr.json); the rise appears 3100->4000 after the episodes have desynchronized (Train/mean_episode_length 322-363 at 2999-3048, ~1390 at 3049-3098, ~1990 by 3900; command-pool fractions halved only during 2999-3098). So the restart did not directly produce the jump. What it cannot exclude: a one-off perturbation (buffer reset, synchronized spawn-pose batch, new RNG stream) that kicked the optimizer onto a different trajectory with delayed payoff. The tooling has no non-restarted continuation to compare against (every resume restarts episodes); only a from-scratch 6000-iteration run would separate 'more iterations' from 'restart + more iterations'.
- **[rules-it-out] (c) eval noise / manifest resolution at the class boundary; marginal completions**: No head read is marginal. Smallest distance to a FAST line at any B head: s15 L 0.22-hold +0.050 (0.2047 vs 0.155) and 0.25-hold +0.059; every other hand >= +0.056. Smallest completion rise +0.055 (s15) against the 0.03 margin and the <= 0.005 cross-manifest spread cited in CHARTER.md:421; within-eval bootstrap 90 % CIs of the hold medians (n = 99-100 episodes) are +/-0.002-0.003. Along-command classes agree with feet at all 36 reads; no wrong_way or static episodes. The one marginal read is descriptive only: s15 model_4000 L at 0.165/0.184 (+0.010/+0.009). Caveat on single-checkpoint heads: s11's hands fell -0.013/-0.020 between model_5000 and the head and s15's R dipped to 0.2347 at 5000 - not near a class line, but the head is one checkpoint, not a plateau read.
- **[weakens] (d) selection: the five forks are all five C_lr B screen seeds, but these seeds were also the step-3 screen seeds**: No selection among forks: all five C_lr B seeds (11-15, fixed by the step-3 design before any result) were forked, so 5/5 FAST/FAST at the head is a whole-population count for those seeds, and the x6k read is a new measurement at a different iteration rather than a re-use of the step-3 statistic. Residual issues: (i) the 4/4 'completes' rate is conditional on heads that were already one-FAST with the second hand rising (SLOW->INT 2500->2999 in all four), so it is 'completion given formation under way', not B's unconditional 6000-iteration rate; (ii) B was chosen over A for extension on the step-3 count (1/5 vs 0/5), so 'B specifically' vs 'any bound-loss arm' is untested; (iii) the C0 control is n = 1 (s11). None of these inflates the within-fork reading.
- **[open] (e) would a from-scratch 6000-iteration B run reproduce this (same-seed determinism, schedules keyed on max_iterations)?**: Same-seed determinism applies to identical trajectories; a fork is not one: the env episodes restart at 2999 and the sampling stream diverges from a continuous run, so a from-scratch seed-13 run at 6000 would be a new sample, not a replay of head+fork. On schedules the news is good: the priv_reg ramp is keyed on the restored update counter (ppo_with_extractor.py:933-938, [0, 0.1, 2000, 3000]; identical slope 3.5e-5/it before and after the fork, 0.1 reached at 4759 in all six forks), the bound ramp is 0.0001:0.001:500 on the counter, and nothing in train.py / the runner keys a schedule to max_iterations (it is passed only as num_learning_iterations; tot_iter is used for logging) - so a from-scratch 6000 run sees the same schedules as head+fork. Expectation: likely to reproduce in kind, but no run demonstrates it; the result is strictly 'the step-3 heads, continued 3000 iterations, complete'.
- **[serious] (f) result specific to the C_lr bed (two side directions + stand) vs the full omni pool**: The forks trained on KRABBY_OMNI_CMD_POOL stand:0.2,side_l:0.4,side_r:0.4 with OMNI_SLOTS 0.2:0.8:0:0 (candidates_v4.yaml:1133-1160); tfevents turn_frac_actual and move_turn_frac_actual are 0.0 throughout.
