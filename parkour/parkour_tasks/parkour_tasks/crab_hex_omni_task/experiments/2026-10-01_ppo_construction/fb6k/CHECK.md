## Confounds

- **[weakens] (a) Seed count: 3 v 3 and what the Fisher p licenses**: 3/3 vs 0/3 gives two-sided Fisher p = 2/C(6,3) = 0.10 (one-sided 0.05); 11.4 PASS is defined as a count rule, not a significance test, and CHARTER 4.5 itself treats 4/5 vs 0/5 (p = 0.048) as the UNLOCK corner, so this PASS is formally weaker than an UNLOCK. What strengthens it beyond the count: the effect is not at the class boundary (B second-hand margins over the FAST line +0.022 to +0.031 m/s at 0.22 and +0.022 to +0.042 at 0.25; C0 hands 0.045-0.073 below the SLOW line), the FB6k C0 heads sit in the same frozen regime as every prior C0 run (x6k/RESULTS.md:20,29: C0 x6k 0.086/0.082; RESULTS.md:55-59: C_lr C0 0/5 SLOW/SLOW), and B's 3/3 here plus x6k's 5/5 (forks) are consistent. What it does not license: a population rate for B (3 seeds, CI on 3/3 is wide), nor a claim about the clock-free pipeline. It licenses 'the pre-registered precondition for P1 is met', which is all 11.4 claims.
- **[rules-it-out] (b) Is the suite 'ratio' the right fwd/back statistic, and do C0's low ratios come from saturation or survival?**: ratio = mean along-command velocity / commanded speed over the hold (metrics.py:651-678; travel frame per metrics.py:839-851), median over the 100 own-hold episodes. Survival is NOT the driver: recomputing the median over surviving episodes only moves C0 fwd 0.691 -> 0.687, 0.579 -> 0.594, 0.645 -> 0.650 and back 0.715 -> 0.713, 0.605 -> 0.612, 0.687 -> 0.693 (metrics/episode_*.json). The deficit is motion quality: C0's mean body speed on fwd is 0.20-0.22 m/s (motion.speed_abs_mean_m_s) but its net travel speed is only 0.146-0.173 and its heading wanders (tracking.wz mean_abs 0.16-0.19 rad/s vs B 0.10-0.12; heading_error_max p90 0.30-0.40 rad vs B 0.14-0.16; raw-path check: only 69 % of C0 0.5-s windows within 20 deg of the command vs 96-100 % for B). That pattern sits on top of the saturated construction (sat1_cam 0.98, sat1_rod 0.96, effective_std at the 2.0 clamp, Loss/entropy 38.0 vs B 20.3). So the ratio is a fair along-command speed statistic here and C0's low values are real slow/weaving walking, not fall artefacts; but the 11.4 rule compares means of medians with a 0.03 margin, which with n = 3 and seed spreads of 0.06-0.11 is a weak instrument in general -- the gap found (0.27-0.33) is simply far larger than that.
- **[weakens] (c) The diag read (untrained): is it diagonal travel at the commanded speed, or a degenerate behaviour?**: B's diag is genuine diagonal travel: commanded travel_yaw 0.84 rad at 0.25 m/s; per-episode mean velocity direction error median 0.016-0.097 rad (max 0.16), net travel speed 0.206-0.241 m/s, cross-track ratio 0.014-0.089, and in the raw root_pos_w paths 91-100 % of 0.5-s windows (median per episode) point within 20 deg of the command while 0-2 % point along the fwd or side axes, so it is not fwd/side zig-zagging; gait_hz 0.36-0.38 locked to clock_hz, tripod 0.53-0.67.
