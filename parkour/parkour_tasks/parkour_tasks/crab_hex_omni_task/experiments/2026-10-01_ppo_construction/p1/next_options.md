# P1-B6 after the t3 stop: ranked next steps (fable·high, workflow wf_4c16e6f4-60b)

## Reading

**Where P1-B6 stands.** The pre-registered t3 stop fired on formation (`p1/t3_gate.json`: B a FAST hand in 1/3, weaker hand below INT in 2/3; C0 SLOW/SLOW 3/3; CHARTER.md:1316-1331). But the diagnosis changes what that STOP means in two ways:

1. **Side formation on t3 is slower, not blocked, and it is more than dilution.** All six B hands rise from model_4500 (s16 +0.022/+0.019, s17 +0.040/+0.013, s18 +0.059/+0.029; `p1/gate/P1B6__B_t3_s*{,_m4500}_lr.json`), C0 is flat, yet at equal per-hand exposure (t3 head ≈ 990 hand-its vs FB6k 4500 ≈ 900) t3's best hands are 0.147/0.150/0.188 against FB6k's 0.210/0.232/0.212. So something in the t3 recipe (turn 0.2 + FACING/YAW_W, fwd .25, back .08) costs more than its share of samples. The data cannot say which ingredient; no FB+turn-only or t3-minus-turn arm exists.

2. **B's turning does not fail in training; it fails on the eval's out-of-band 90-degree hold.** Training yaw_progress is B 0.023-0.028 vs C0 0.011-0.019 at 5500-5999, reward_tracking_yaw is higher for B, σ is flat at 0.78-0.81 (no exploration collapse), KL and LR match C0. On turn_l90/r90 (face 1.5708, 12 s; `experiments/eval/scenarios_omni.yaml:191-192`) B makes one 0.15-0.19 rad burst then steps in place (145-486 steps/rad vs C0 50-70), while the facing obs is still pinned at its 0.6 clip. The whole P1 chain trains facing offsets of 0.2-0.6 rad only (`candidates_v6.yaml:1768, 1797, 1822, 1847, 1872`); C0-type heads generalise to 1.57 rad because they turn with saturated ±1 bang-bang (|μ| ≈ 8, lock ≈ 0.7) that simply keeps going while the sign says "turn", and the facing clip was added for exactly that failure on a 1c head (`crab_hex_omni_phases.py:173-184`: the 90-degree canary fell while the in-band 0.5-rad diagnostic settled 16/16). B, with |μ| ≲ 1.1 and weak in-place cam drive (0.29-0.40 vs 0.7-1.1 on fwd/move_turn), has no such free generalisation. Whether B turns inside its training band, and whether its stochastic policy keeps rotating, are both undecided and both testable for ≈ 1 GPU-h on existing heads (`eval_crab_hex_gait.py:109-114` has `--sample-actions`, diagnostic only).

**What this does and does not implicate.**
- *Not the bound:* every C0 executed action lies in [-1, 1] below the 1.1 hinge; B's share of |μ| > 1.1 on turn holds (cam 0.01-0.07) is below its own fwd holds.
- *Not exploration:* σ 0.78-0.81, entropy 21 vs C0's σ=2 maximum 38, exec_change 5× C0. More entropy is not indicated.
- *Not action_rate for turning:* exec_action_rate is 2× C0 with or without turning in the pool (3.0 vs 1.45 on t3; 2.9 vs 1.3 on FB6k); turn jitter is 1.35× C0, under the §12.5 1.5× flag (CHARTER.md:698-701). And A (same construction, action_rate −0.3) stalls the second hand as σ decays and the rods re-saturate (RESULTS.md:110-113), so restoring it before formation would spend the side result.
- *Does implicate:* the training facing band vs the eval band (config, not code), and possibly co-formation of turn and side strokes in one net from scratch, which the data cannot separate from dilution.

**The P1 final gate as written would COST B on turn** (facing_closed margin 0.03, yaw_progress 0.01; CHARTER.md:684-688) if the t3 turn read carried through the tails unchanged: B 0.04-0.17 vs C0 0.39-0.55 is "clearly inferior" by a wide margin, and W reads 0.787/0.102 (`policy/manifest.yaml:245`). move_turn is reported, not gated (CHARTER.md:690-692); W itself has 0.365 survival and "nobody actually turns during move_turn" (manifest.yaml:233, 251-262), so B's 0.00-0.17 is a bigger gap than W's but on a hold the pipeline already fails.

**Assets in hand.** Three FB6k B heads are FAST/FAST with both hands formed, |μ| 0.5/0.88, σ 0.75-0.77, fall share 0 (fb6k/RESULTS.md:15-21; CHECK.md:23); they have never seen turn, FACING or YAW_W and are clock-visible base 1b (1a/1b differ only in DIR_BAND, SLOTS, RSI_SOURCE, which the tail overlays override; `crab_hex_omni_phases.py:266-271`, p1_design "Base 1a vs FB's 1b"). Forks continue σ, the bound ramp and the priv_reg counter (CHARTER.md:355-367, 611; the x6k forks completed 4/4, x6k/RESULTS.md:24-25). The tail tooling (per-stage `--lr-eval`, `RESUME_READ=1`, `STOP_ON_REF_STOP`, c6 pre-cut read) exists (CHARTER.md:1233-1239, 696-707).

**Honest limits.** n = 3 everywhere. The side-first order (option 2) reverses the user's adopted turn-first C1d order, whose benefit was measured on incumbent-type nets (turning 0.42→0.62, memory omni-task-campaign-active), not on B; whether B turns better when turn arrives after the stroke is unknown. The C0 FB6k heads are a weak control for option 2 because they have no stroke to lose (p1_design, Alternatives 1).
