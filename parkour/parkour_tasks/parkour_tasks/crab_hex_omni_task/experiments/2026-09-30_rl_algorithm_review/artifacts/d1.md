## 1. The problems this review targets

Restated from the dossier (its section numbers) so this document stands alone:

1. **Bang-bang control.** On the omni head of record W, 97.0 % of pre-clip mean components have |μ| > 1
   (median 11.2); all 303 evaluated checkpoints at 999+ iterations are 91–100 % saturated; 71 % by iteration
   199 (§3.3). Signature: 2.23 sign flips per joint-second at stand, stand stepping, the rigid 3-feet pattern, an
   `action_rate` cost of 0.95 at |μ| = 0 vs 0.00 at |μ| = 9. The only σ experiment, M1 (cap 0.5 on a fine-tune
   of saturated X08:c6, no bound on μ), was REJECTED with jitter z −2.7 to −6.4 (§4.3).
2. **σ pinned.** `std.clamp(0.05, 2.0)` (`crab_actor_critic_with_encoder.py:50`) passes zero gradient above
   2.0; σ climbs from `init_noise_std` 1.5 to the cap in 59/59 fresh runs of ≥ 3500 iterations and never leaves
   (§3.2). `entropy_coef` 0.01 in 436/437 runs; no entropy-0 control.
3. **The KL controller is the real learning rate.** The rsl_rl per-minibatch rule (÷1.5 if KL > 0.02, ×1.5 if
   KL < 0.005, floor 1e-5) runs 80× per iteration on 384-sample minibatches; the configured 3e-4 is erased
   within iteration 0 in 240/254 fresh runs; the LR is at the floor in 62.9 % of iterations; on W only 11–16 %
   of the KL it reacts to changes any executed action, vs 70–79 % during formation (§3.1). KL, clip fraction
   and explained variance are not logged.
4. **Inherited geometry.** 256 envs × 24 steps = 6,144 samples, 5 × 16 minibatches of 384, copied from a
   6,144-env Go2 config (147,456). Wall time is latency-bound (4× envs = +5 %; GPU 61–69 % at 66 W, 4.4 GB);
   no run above 256 envs (§3.5). γ 0.99 / λ 0.95: 0.34 s GAE horizon inside a 0.48 s rollout on a 1.2–2.8 s gait.
5. **Seed variance ≈ effect size.** Turning blocks R_seed/R_eval 3.19/0.97 and 4.12/0.94; decided effects
   cleared the seed term by 1.05–4.3×; tripod formed in 1/4 plain-clock seeds, 0/7 alternated in the basin
   search; 2 of 4 same-seed pairs diverge from iteration 20 (§4.1, D18).
6. **Basins.** The forward unison glide out-earns the target under the same reward (28.69 vs 26.15 at 3k);
   every other degenerate family earns less, i.e. basin selection (§4.4(a)).
7. **Two-direction side walking.** Two-hand networks are SLOW or DEAD (0.028–0.103 m/s); +y-only heads are
   FAST (0.184–0.217 m/s) and the exact M_y canonicaliser makes them fast both ways; a fresh net distils the
   two-hand policy to 99 % sign agreement; PPO settings were byte-identical across arms (§4.4(c)).
8. **Value side.** Clipped value loss; one Adam group for actor + critic + std; no obs or return normalisation;
   reward clipped at ≥ 0 (13.6 % of return forgiven on W; teacher value loss ≤ 0.0003); a flat critic priced
   ~0.5/step against ~0.0015/step on the bridge; critic reset fixed one hand-off, hurt B1 (§4.6).
9. **Symmetry.** Mirror LOSS only, 0.5 on raw unclipped means; augmentation never enabled; seed-1 gains
   (0.573 vs 0.032 at 3k) that did not replicate (E1 seeds 0.541/0.0/0.026/0.0) (§4.5).
10. **Constraints.** rsl-rl-lib 5.0.1 only; one shared 16 GB GPU; 1,149-dim obs (4.6 KB/transition); every
    consumer needs the estimator/history-encoder modules; the ±1 clip is load-bearing (teacher fell 100/100
    without it); the student regresses raw teacher means (§6).

**The short answer.** The literature indicts the construction, not PPO. No reference legged stack pairs a
binding ±1 clip with an unbounded mean and a clamped σ ≥ the bound (§2); the saturation is a documented
clipped-Gaussian pathology in which lowering σ alone cannot restore an interior mean (§2–§3); the
per-minibatch KL rule runs 64–96× below the minibatch it was tuned in (§6); batch and γ/λ were inherited (§7).
The fixes are inside PPO and cost tens of GPU-hours. Where the literature disagrees with the dossier is on
what they buy: geometry and smoothness change reliably, task return only inconsistently, and near-bang-bang
may be close to what this objective asks for (§4). Off-policy and model-based families are slower or more
seed-variable in the closest analogues (§10); the two-direction failure has no precedent and its
best-supported test is canonical training (§8); no verdict should rest on fewer than 3 seeds (§11).

## 2. The clipped-Gaussian pathology and the reference stacks (question a, part 1)

**Key papers:** Chou et al. 2017 [1]; Hsu et al. 2020 [2]; He et al. 2026 [3]; Geiß et al. 2024 [4];
legged_gym [5]; extreme-parkour [6]; Isaac Lab cfgs, PR #2019 [7]; PR #984 [8]; Raffin [9]; IsaacGymEnvs [10];
Isaac Lab Ant rl_games cfg [11]; rl_games [12]; VideoMimic [13].

Env-side clipping replicates the reward at the bound and "bias[es] the estimated policy distribution toward
the boundary"; "as σ grows, bias also increases" [1]. Once the executed action is flat in μ, by analogy with
Hsu's no-signal region, "the mean and the standard deviation ... fluctuate randomly due to noise" [2].
Entropy on the latent Gaussian has zero gradient on the mean and a constant upward push on σ [3], so
`entropy_coef` 0.01 drives σ to the cap, where the clamp zeroes every gradient. Measured (SB3 PPO, 3 seeds,
80-muscle MyoLeg): a clipped Gaussian executed 89.07 % of actions within 5 % of a bound, 82.12 % of
state-conditioned means lay outside the interval, and with zero variance 83.83 % were still near a bound;
among matched tanh policies near-bound occupancy was 71.42 % with latent entropy, 29.76 % with none, 18.83 %
with entropy on the executed action, whose Jacobian "pulls the mean inward"; "if P10 is already large,
reducing variance alone cannot produce an interior mean" [3]. A GAIL/TRPO musculoskeletal study saw the same
drift ("the predicted mean arbitrarily far out-of-bounds ... while not reducing or even increasing its
standard deviation"), no gain from target entropy, gains from mean-regularising objectives (~+200 for a
flipped-uniform KL on running), and bounded heads that "did not pay dividends" [4].

No reference stack operates here. legged_gym: clip ±100, action_scale 0.5, init std 1.0, entropy 0.01, 4 ×
24,576 [5]. Extreme-parkour (Krabby's parent): clip 1.2 with action_scale 0.25 for its default `a1` task
(±4.8 policy units), unclamped std 1.0, entropy 0.01, optional `tanh_encoder_output`, 4 × 36,864 [6]. Isaac
Lab velocity tasks: scale 0.5, `clip_actions` None, init std 1.0, entropy 0.0025–0.01, 4 × 24,576; the
wrapper clip (PR #2019, after the unmerged #984) exists to break a last_action → critic-error →
larger-action loop [7, 8]; a trained A1 policy "uses less than 5 % of the action space" of its ±100 box [9].
rl_games clips at ±1 with std 1 (binding on samples) but bounds the mean with a quadratic loss inside ±1.1
(AnymalPPO `bounds_loss_coef` 0.001, `kl_threshold` 0.008, minibatch 32,768, entropy 0; Ant 1e-4);
AnymalTerrainPPO turns both off [10, 11, 12].

**What it means here.** Krabby's velocity-target cams make ±1 the max-speed command, so its clip binds by
construction with σ 1.5–2.0 against it and no mean bound; every safeguard the reference stacks use is absent.
With 97 % of means outside ±1, P10 ≥ 97 %, so by [3] M1's rejection is the predicted outcome, not evidence
against lower σ. Cheap audit first: do the last-action and history observations carry the raw (|μ| ~ 10–30) or
the clipped action, the loop PR #2019 was written to break [7]?
