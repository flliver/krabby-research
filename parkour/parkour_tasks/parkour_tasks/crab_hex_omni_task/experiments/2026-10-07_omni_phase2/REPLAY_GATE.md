## Episode_Reward basis (CHARTER §8 term liveness, 3× tolerance)

rsl_rl's `Episode_Reward/<term>` is the mean episode sum of w·value·dt divided by episode_length_s (40 s). The replay estimate is w · Σ_c mix_c · rate_c · L/T_max. L/T_max = 1827 / 2000 = 0.914, from B's c6x `Train/mean_episode_length`, mean of the last 200 iterations. The mix is the c6x pool `stand:0.15,fwd:0.14,side_l:0.09,side_r:0.09,back:0.09,omni:0.14,turn:0.1,fwd_turn:0.06,side_l_turn:0.035,side_r_turn:0.035,omni_turn:0.07` (omni → diag), with stand set to B's logged `stand_frac_actual` 0.127 and the rest rescaled:

| category | stand | fwd | side | back | diag | turn | move_turn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mix | 0.127 | 0.144 | 0.185 | 0.092 | 0.144 | 0.103 | 0.205 |
| E1 raw /s (B) | 385.9 | 437.5 | 410.3 | 412.3 | 407.9 | 504.8 | 476.6 |

- **E1**: -0.00011 × 434.2 W × 0.914 = **-0.04364**.
- **F1**: 0.90 × 0.127 × 0.4086 × 0.914 = **0.04276**. This is at B's current stand income. At statue-level stillness it would be 0.08344, still inside 3×.
