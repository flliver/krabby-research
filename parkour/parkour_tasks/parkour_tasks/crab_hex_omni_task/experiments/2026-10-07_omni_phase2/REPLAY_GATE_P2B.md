- `reward_foot_clearance` (`reward_foot_clearance_terrain`): w · mean over the six feet of in_swing · clamp(relu(h − 0.03), max 0.26) · gate.
- `penalty_swing_min_clearance` (`penalty_swing_min_clearance_terrain`): w · mean over feet of in_swing · [h < 0.04] · gate.
- Here h = foot_z − terrain_z under the foot, in_swing = ‖contact force‖ ≤ 0.1 N, and gate = max(‖cmd_xy‖, 1.2·|cmd_wz|) > 0.12 (the `_cmd_gate` omni branch). No parkour_flat mask (FOOT_CLEAR_FLAT 1).
- `reward_orientation` → `reward_orientation_upright`: w · (g_x² + g_y²) of projected_gravity_b, every tile.

**Windows.**
- **Walking:** reward steps whose command hold is not `stand`/`settle`, with the command gate on (turn holds count, because a turn command gates the CL terms on under omni), and outside the 2 s before a fall.
- **Pre-fall:** for a `crab_failure` episode, the 100 reward steps (2 s) before the failing step.
- **Shares:** |w| · Σ kernel / Σ tracking over a head's walking steps, a ratio of sums over both field rows, both env_seeds and the suite.

**Fidelity.** The real landed twins (`omni_clearance.reward_foot_clearance_terrain` / `penalty_swing_min_clearance_terrain`, with the library `_cmd_gate` exec'd from source) and the exec'd `reward_orientation_upright` were run on recorded s18 field_hard steps. ## CL: terrain-relative lift

### (a) Income rises with terrain-relative swing apex, pit swings included

Swings are `p2_mech.extract_swings` (a no-contact run at ≤ 5 N bounded by contact). Apex is max(foot_z − terrain_z). A swing is a pit swing when it lifts off more than 0.03 m below the other feet's terrain. Each swing's income is the foot's own share of the term over the swing span, 1/6 per foot, with dt and the gate. Only walking liftoffs are counted. The rule is Spearman ρ > 0 in both groups.

## Caveats

- **Terrain sample.** The raws' terrain_z is a downward raycast of the terrain mesh. The live twin samples the height field at the nearest pixel (0.08 m) of the tile the foot is on. They agree on block tops and pit floors and can differ within about one pixel of a vertical wall or pit edge. The fidelity check substitutes only this sample.
- **Contact.** The reward's swing test is ‖force‖ ≤ 0.1 N on the recorded `foot_force_norm` (the same sensor read). p2_mech's swing span uses 5 N; it is used only to delimit swings for check (a).
- **Distribution.** Eval schedules (8 holds per field episode; one category per suite episode) are not the training command distribution. The shares are eval-walking shares, and the Episode_Reward mix is an estimate. The calibration table bounds the error.
- **TG liveness margin.** The flat-tile tilt cost replays at 0.35–0.59× the S1 runs' own training tail. Training has push and mass randomisation and stochastic actions; the evals have neither. If the live TG term runs high by the same factor, P2__S1_s18 would log about -0.0258 against the stored -0.0112, which is 2.3× (p2_live's limit is 3×). A false liveness FAIL at +200 is possible there and should be read against this table. The same factor puts TG's training share near 3.6 % at the chosen weight, inside the 5 % ceiling.
- **Tilt concentrates in move_turn.** At the chosen weight the move_turn holds' TG share is move_turn 4.1 %, move_turn_r 5.0 % of their tracking, against about 0.2–1.1 % on the other holds. This is descriptive: the bands are read on all walking steps.
- **Heads.** These are the p2a heads, which never trained with the p2b terms. A policy trained under CL will move these shares, which is what the +200 liveness read and the s18 stop exist for.
- **field_hard env_seeds.** Only env_seeds 1 and 2 exist for the p2a heads. Seeds 3 and 4 arrive with the step-3 REF evals.
