## Caveats

- The eval rolls out the deterministic mean; the sampled pair (REF, noisy statue) is the training-time statistic and is gated only for anti-termination.
- `reward_collision` reads only the Hip / Femur links (`leg_contact_force`); the trunk contact is not recorded. `reward_feet_stumble` is not replayable from force norms. Neither fires on a still upright stand.
- The window is the steady stand hold of a 12-s eval hold after a 2-s settle; training stand slots also begin mid-stride, which this window does not contain.
