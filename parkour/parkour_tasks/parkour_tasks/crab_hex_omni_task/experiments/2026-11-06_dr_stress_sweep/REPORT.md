### 3.2 Mechanism (raw stamps, verifier corrections applied; correlational — no retrained policy was run)

- **Rods sit at the slew cap almost always.** The slew is kinematic and load-blind (`rod_speed` is the commanded rod-state slew, not measured motion). At nominal (suite own holds, es1, share of steps with |rod_speed| ≥ 0.95 × cap) the hip is at cap on 0.96–0.995 of steps and the knee on 0.85–0.92; across levels and loads (fwd, stance and swing) the hip stays ≥ 0.96 and the knee ranges 0.78 (×1.35 swing) to 0.93 (×0.7 L090); max |rod_speed| equals the scaled cap (0.0196 / 0.0231 m/s at ×0.7). A lower cap slows every leg motion one for one.
- **The policy does not re-time its gait.** Fwd cam cadence stays at 1.68–1.72 rad/s from ×0.7 to ×1.35 at both loads. Slower lift means lower swing clearance (fwd L090 0.103 → 0.084 m at ×0.7), more support feet (diag 3.06 → 3.58) and a body ≈ 2 cm lower.
- **Stance knees climb onto the 400 N·m holding cap.** Suite fwd-hold stance knee cap saturation (share of (leg, step) stance samples at ≥ 0.95 × 400 N·m, es1) at L090 is 0.30 / 0.37 / 0.41 at ×1.0 / 0.8 / 0.7 (diag mid-row 0.28 / 0.54 / 0.67). Over the 18 es1 cells of T00/T01/T02, completion tracks cap saturation (Spearman −0.88 mid-row, −0.83 all legs) and support count (−0.89), which cannot be told apart. **Correction to the "mid-row channel":** in the last second before a T01 fall (field_light both seeds, suite es2) front/rear knee saturation rises and support is lost (student 2.7–2.9 feet vs 3.4–3.5 in alive control windows) while mid-row saturation stays at its control level; the suite es1 window is the exception (mid 0.59 → 0.73, rear 0.31 → 0.45, front 0.16 → 0.11), so the row-specific reading is not settled. T02 falls differ: all rows saturated, support not reduced; swing-knee saturation is ≈ 1e-3 at nominal and up to 0.35 in T02 cells.
- **Falls are late nose-down pitch-outs at the 0.5 rad total-tilt limit** (code-confirmed, `parkour_mdp_cfg.py:1377`): suite median ≈ 10.4–11.1 s into the 12-s hold, all `pitch_fwd` (a travel-relative class name, not the pitch sign). In field_light, "late" holds for diag and move_turn only; back-hold falls include onset falls at the diag → back switch (es2 p25 0.3 s).
- **Not purely stability.** Speed parity between bases holds in fwd and lateral but not diag: at ×0.7 diag reaches 0.218 m/s at L000 vs 0.190 at L090 (ratio 0.87 vs 0.76), so load also costs diag speed.

### 3.4 Recommended range and where it is trained

- **U(0.6, 1.35) per env per episode**, hip and knee slew scaled together (as swept). C §3.3 requires covering the failing-side band edge (×0.7), never capped at the tolerated edge (none here): U(0.7, 1.35) is the minimum; 0.6 keeps ×0.7 off the training edge (inference). Not 0.5: ×0.5 collapses at L090 (0.03) and is a speed-capability fail at L000, so it risks infeasible training (inference; review [58]). Ramp the low side in (review [39]). Drawing slower rods at heavier payloads is the physical coupling (inference). Nothing is needed above 1.35.
- **Where:** teacher training, then re-distil the student; entry point and gate in decision 3.
- **Code:** `KRABBY_STRESS_ROD_SPEED` is a process-wide scalar (`stress_knobs.py`); training needs a default-off per-env scale drawn at reset (moves code_sha). No observation carries rod speed today: the current-sense gate normalises by 0.3 × the unscaled rated speed and clamps to 1, so a capped rod reads the same at ×0.7. Whether to add a priv_latent slot is open (decision 3).

### 3.5 The plant-model holding-force question

| Stance rod force, suite fwd hold (alive, foot > 20 N) | 0 kg p50 / p90 | 90 kg p50 / p90 | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knee (Yuhuang YH8-523D) | 2.73 / 5.37 kN | 3.34 / 5.41 kN | 500 N |
| Hip (Sunline SLA08-24) | 2.98 / 5.92 kN | 4.34 / 7.56 kN | 2000 N |

Force = |applied_torque| / linkage moment arm (my recompute, `writer/rodf.py`, every 4th env; agrees with the actuators verifier). Knee p90+ is the sim's holding cap (400 N·m / ≈ 0.074 m ≈ 5.4 kN; `effort_limit_sim` "at holding level", `crab_hex_scene_cfg.py`); rated forces are never enforced — drive force exists only as the slew. Measured against live rod motion, **only ≈ 8–10 % of stance steps are driving steps** (corrected from "about half"), so the question is **static holding / backdrive capacity** of a self-locking screw at kN loads, which no DR range settles. Bounds: `applied_torque` is Isaac Lab's PD estimate, and the knee follows only ≈ 0.3 of its commanded rod motion in fwd stance, so part of this may be PD internal force rather than gravity load (not yet separated); the stand-height campaign's foot-load statics gave 1.0–1.5 kN standing (`2026-10-13_stand_height/REPORT.md` §5). Both bounds are 2–7× the rating. The current proxy sits at its 1.5×-rated clip in ≈ 47 % of samples. Consequence: a force-dependent speed line built from the datasheet stall (review §2, [6, 9]) would stall the knee in nearly every driving stance step; bench data first (decision 5).

No field_hard / walls-hard rows, steps, gaps, tables, stand-up, or the stand-height network (only the student's stand slot).
