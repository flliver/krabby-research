## `KRABBY_AIRTIME_W` (de-weight of `reward_feet_air_time_positive`, +0.8)

| category | income share at 0.8, as trained (float32 sensor clock) | income share at 0.8, exact timing (counterfactual) |
|---|---|---|
| fwd | 0.0 % | 1.5 % |
| side | 0.0 % | 1.2 % |
| back | 0.0 % | 1.6 % |
| diag | 0.0 % | 1.3 % |
| move_turn | 0.0 % | 1.6 % |

Even with exact timing the income does not pay the defect: its per-second rho against `contact_switch_hz` is fwd -0.30, side -0.40, back -0.38, diag -0.28, move_turn -0.09 -- it pays LONG swings (> 0.38 s), which a chattering foot does not make. Its per-m / per-rad rho against swings per m/rad (Gate 1 table) is mostly the shared 1/distance denominator.

Proposal: 0.8 -> **0.4** (halves the air-time income). **But the term is inert in training.** `ContactSensor.compute_first_contact(dt)` tests `current_contact_time < dt + 1e-8`, and the sensor's float32 `_timestamp` (reset each episode, += 0.005 per substep) makes the per-refresh elapsed time 0.0200001-0.0200005 s once the episode is ~2 s old, so a touchdown after that never registers as a first contact and the term pays 0. The replay reproduces this bit-for-bit (`sensor_elapsed_f32`), and the X08:c6 training log agrees (cross-check below). A de-weight therefore changes nothing: M4 is effectively `KRABBY_FOOT_SLIP_W` alone. The exact-timing column is what the term WOULD pay if the timing were fixed -- a separate decision (fixing it would switch ON a +0.8 swing income the current gait never trained with).

## How each term was replayed

Row `t` of the eval npz is recorded BEFORE `env.step(actions[t])`, so env step `s` reads actions and command from row `s` and the post-physics state from row `s + 1` (the state the reward manager reads). Integrals are `sum value * dt` over the own hold's whole window (`report._window_mask`, steady = all). Actions are the stored pre-clip policy mean clipped to +-1.0 (`clip_actions`), as the runner's wrapper does.

| term (knob) | training function | replayed as |
|---|---|---|
| power (`KRABBY_POWER_W`) | `penalty_mechanical_power` | sum over all 24 joints of abs(tau x qdot) |
| smooth2 (`KRABBY_ACTION_SMOOTH2_W`) | `penalty_action_second_diff` | squared norm of (a_s - 2 a_(s-1) + a_(s-2)), undelayed clipped history, 0 for s < 2 |
| joint_vel (`KRABBY_JOINT_VEL_W`) | `penalty_joint_vel_l2` | sum of qdot^2 over the 12 rod joints |
| action_rate(_sq) (`KRABBY_ACTION_RATE_W` / `_SQ`) | `reward_action_rate` | norm (squared) of r_s - r_(s-1), r_s = a_(s-1) (the delayed `raw_actions`), zero at reset |
| foot_slip (`KRABBY_FOOT_SLIP_W`) | `penalty_loaded_foot_slip` | sum of planar foot speed over feet with force > 50 N |
| stand_pose (`KRABBY_STAND_POSE_W`) | `penalty_stand_pose_l1` | sum abs(q - q_default) over the rod joints, stand hold only (the eval analogue of `stand_mask`) |
| airtime (`KRABBY_AIRTIME_W`) | `reward_feet_air_time_positive` | contact-sensor air/contact clocks re-run per refresh (contact > 1 N), float32 timing as in training; `airtime_exact` = exact timing |
| foot_idle (stock) | `PenaltyFootIdleWhenForward` | idle-step counter from reset (contact > 0.1 N), relu(idle - 90), omni `_cmd_gate` |
| task income | stock kernels + omni facing / yaw progress | as trained (std / weights above); facing reads `heading_err` of row s + 1 |

Mismatches that cannot be removed offline: the eval is the deterministic mean (training samples at sigma 2.0 -- the state-driven terms cannot be re-simulated under noise; the action terms are Monte-Carlo'd above); the eval's fixed 12-s holds are not the training slot pool; the stand gate is the eval's stand hold; the eval harness's `KRABBY_TRACK_SIGMA2 0.1` env var is an eval-env setting and is ignored -- the replay prices the TRAINING kernels.
