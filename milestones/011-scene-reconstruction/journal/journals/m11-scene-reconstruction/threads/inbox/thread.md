# Inbox

Drop notes here when you don't yet know which thread they belong to. Move them into the right thread (and update any `consolidates_notes:` lists) when consolidating into entries.

Per the journal convention, every journal has an `inbox` thread. Don't let it accumulate forever — sweep it during entry-writing sessions.
